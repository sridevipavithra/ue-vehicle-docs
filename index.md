# Vehicle Amphibious for Unreal Engine

Turn any Chaos wheeled vehicle into an amphibious car: it floats, changes into a boat with folding wheels, and drives back onto land. Works with any water system, multiplayer ready, with a demo map to tune in. Unreal Engine 5.8; Windows, macOS, Android and iOS.

[User guide](VehicleAmphibious.md): setup, the demo map, water, tuning, the vehicle HUD, multiplayer, troubleshooting

A C++ reference for programmers is included with the plugin, in its `Docs` folder.
